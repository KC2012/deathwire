HOW TO STAY ALIVE

Your bike cruises automatically. Throttle and boost build speed; braking changes the approach to a hazard. Lean on the ground to wheelie or press the front wheel down; lean in the air to rotate and land wheels-down. Hard nose-first landings cost health and speed. Launch ramps and hops can clear gaps, spikes, and machinery. In DEATH RACE your position is ranked against riders on the same course. In GAUNTLET you get three checkpoint recoveries.

Track geometry and hazard animation teach you how to survive. Full loops automatically engage their guide rails when approached at speed. Warning signs mark trap sections. Riders telegraph attacks; strike forward at close range. Fragile road breaks under EVERY rider. Near misses, clean airtime and physical takedowns build combo score. Death Races have real finishing places; Gauntlet has checkpoints. Tip: Jump before saws and spike beds. Attacking near explosives can set off a chain reaction.
